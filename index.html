Our Story

Baked by neighbors, trusted like family.

Dough Bunny began in a home kitchen with one simple mission: bring back the cookie jar the way it used to be.

No shortcuts, no fillers, no mystery ingredients. Every recipe has been tested, tweaked, and perfected one batch at a time — and every order is still mixed, rolled, and baked by hand.

We're a licensed Florida cottage food operation, so cookies are never sitting on a shelf. They're baked fresh for you, then boxed with a bow.
