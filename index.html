Recipe for a Homemade Cookie

Ingredients

  • Real butter — never shortening
  • Pure vanilla extract
  • Real chocolate & real fruit
  • Whole eggs, no powders
  • A whole lot of love

Directions

  1. Cream the butter and sugar by the batch, never by the ton.
  2. Mix, scoop, roll, and shape every cookie by hand.
  3. Frost and decorate with a steady, loving hand.
  4. Bake fresh — only once your order comes in.
  5. Share with someone you love.

our kitchen

From our home kitchen to yours.

Dough Bunny isn't a factory or a storefront — it's a home kitchen, a well-loved mixing bowl, and a lot of heart.

We started baking the way most of us learned: for family, for friends, for birthdays and bad days and "just because." Every recipe was tested and tweaked at home until it tasted like a hug.

We still bake that way. As a licensed Florida cottage food operation, every order is made from scratch and baked fresh just for you — never mass-produced, never sitting on a shelf.
