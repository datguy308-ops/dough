Our Story

Baked by neighbors,
trusted like family.

Dough Bunny started in a home kitchen with one mission: bring back the cookie jar the way it used to be — full of things you can actually pronounce. No shortcuts, no fillers, no mystery ingredients.

We're a licensed Florida cottage food operation, which means every cookie is baked fresh to order in small batches, tested and perfected recipe by recipe. Real butter, real chocolate, and honest measurements — nothing more, nothing less.
