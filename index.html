Our Story

Baked by neighbors,
trusted like family.

Dough Bunny started in a tiny home kitchen with one mission: bring back the bakery case the way it used to be — full of things you can actually pronounce. No shortcuts, no fillers, no mystery ingredients.

Today we're a local favorite for celebration cakes, morning pastries, and everyday breads, but the standard hasn't changed. Every recipe is built from scratch, every batch is small enough to taste-test, and every ingredient earns its place in the bowl.

Why It Tastes Different

Clean ingredients, no compromises

High-end doesn't mean complicated. It means better butter, better flour, and never cutting a corner to save a dollar.

🌾

Organic & Non-GMO Flour

Stone-milled, unbleached flour sourced from growers we trust — nothing engineered, nothing bleached.

🧈

European-Style Butter

Higher butterfat, slow-cultured, and always real. It's the difference you taste in every flaky layer.

🥚

Local Farm Eggs

Sourced from farms within a short drive, delivered fresh — richer color, richer flavor.

🍯

Naturally Sweetened

Raw cane sugar, wildflower honey, and real vanilla bean — never corn syrup, never artificial flavoring.

Zero Preservatives

🌿

Thoughtfully Sourced

From dairy to chocolate, we choose suppliers who share our standard for clean, honest ingredients.
