Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Dough Bunny is a Florida home bakery. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website or place an order, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, call or text (786) 759-6369 or email doughbunnybakery@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you send an order request, we collect the information you enter:

  • Your name, email address, and (if you give it) phone number
  • Your delivery date, preferred delivery time, delivery address, and delivery instructions
  • Your cookie order and any notes you add — including allergy information, if you choose to share it
  • Whether you agreed to our order policies, and whether you opted in to promotional emails and texts

We don't collect payment card details on this website. Payments are handled by Square (see below).

How we use it

  • To review, confirm, bake, and deliver your order, and to contact you about it
  • To work out your delivery fee from your delivery address
  • To send you a Square payment link once your order is confirmed
  • To send promotional emails and texts — only if you opted in
  • To keep the business records we're required to keep

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. We share it only with the services that help us take and fulfill orders:

  • Square processes payments. When you pay through a Square payment link, Square collects and handles your payment information under its own privacy notice.
  • Google (Gmail) — order requests and messages are received at our Gmail address.
  • Our order-form and maps (distance) services. We're finalizing these and will name them here before they're used.
  • Google Fonts serves this website's fonts, so your browser connects to Google to load them.

We may also share information if required by law.

Promotional emails & texts

We only send promotional emails or text messages if you check the optional box on the order form. Agreeing isn't required to order. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to any text, use the unsubscribe link in any email, or contact us to opt out at any time.

Your cookie box in your browser

The cookies you add to your box are saved in your own browser (local storage) so they're still there if you come back. That information stays on your device; you can clear it anytime by emptying your box or clearing your browser's site data. This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep order information only as long as we need it to fulfill your order, keep our business records, and meet legal requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete the information you've given us (unless we need to keep it for our records or by law) by calling, texting, or emailing us.

Children

Our website isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the "last updated" date above.
